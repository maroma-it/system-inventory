What this form is for

This form is one of the working forms staff complete as part of SDGE - ESA Whole Home (PP/D). It is organized into 30 sections, covering areas such as New Central HVAC System, Water Heater Information, Insulation Area, and Existing Central HVAC System. It connects to two other forms and looks up reference data from three related forms.

Five automated steps watch this form — most run when a saved record changes.

Runs when a 425 - San Diego Installation record is updated, and only if Submission Status is 'Ready for Review'.
Sends an email to configured recipients — subject “SDGE Whole Home Installation Review for {the record's Customer Name} - {the record's Enrollment Number}”.

Marked for Deletion425_MARKED_FOR_DELETION
Runs on a schedule (every Sunday at 5:00), checking 425 - San Diego Installation records, and only if Mark for Deletion is 'True'.
Performs a step of type DeleteFormResponse on 425 - San Diego Installation.
Installation Approved425_INSTALLATION_2
Runs when a 425 - San Diego Installation record is updated, and only if Review Status is 'Approved'.
Sends an email to configured recipients — subject “SDGE Whole Home Installation Approved {the record's Enrollment Number}”.
Review Notification (Operations)425_REVIEW_NOTIFICATION
Runs when a 425 - San Diego Installation record is updated, and only if Review Status compares to 'one of: Corrections Required, Approved' and ReviewerName_c ?.
Creates a new 425 - San Diego Installation record, filling in eight fields automatically.
Sends an email to configured recipients — subject “[Auto-Log] Review Notification | 425 - {the record's Enrollment Number}”.
