What this form is for

Documents and evaluates diagnostic tests performed during a residential home assessment. It includes intake fields for recording results from the Blower Door Test, which measures air leakage and overall envelope tightness; the Duct Test & Sealing, which assesses duct system efficiency and verifies proper sealing; and Natural Gas Appliance Testing, which evaluates the performance, safety, and combustion characteristics of gas-powered appliances. Form 315 ensures accurate data collection to support compliance with program standards and safety protocols. It is organized into 35 sections, covering areas such as Pre-Installation NGAT, Pre-Installation Blower Door Test, Post-Installation Blower Door Test, and Post-Installation Duct Test. It connects to one other form and looks up reference data from one related form.
